## Sustainability Data Support: Illustrative Portfolio

Sample work showing how raw energy, carbon and travel data becomes a clear, management-ready analysis. All data is synthetic and for demonstration only.

## What's inside

workbooks: Excel analysis workbooks, with raw data, clean data, emission factors, calculations, KPIs and a dashboard |
case-studies: Editable Word case studies with charts, insights and recommendations |


## Case studies

1. **Manufacturing: energy and carbon.** Monthly energy use, emissions by source and intensity per unit produced.
2. **University: building operations.** Energy per occupied FTE, which separates real efficiency from changes in occupancy.
3. **University: travel and commuting.** A booking log for business trips plus a commuting survey, combined into one monthly emissions view.

## Excel workbook structure

Each workbook follows the same flow:
Raw Data → Clean Data → Emission Factors → Calculations → KPIs → Dashboard → Client Inputs

Calculations are live formulas, and the assumptions are visible and editable.

## Scope note

This portfolio demonstrates data preparation, screening calculations, KPI development and reporting. It is not independent GHG verification or assurance, a statutory energy audit, legal advice or a guarantee of savings.
